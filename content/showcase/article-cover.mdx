Pick a cover size (Open Graph 1200×630 is a common default). The headline, author, and hero photo are variables, so publishing a post can also publish its share image.

Use this when you want:

- Consistent typography across a publication
- Automatic OG images for docs or a CMS
- Separate crops for WeChat, Twitter, and the site itself
