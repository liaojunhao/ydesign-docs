Keep a catalog template for main images, promotion badges, and price tags. Each SKU is just a variable payload — no designer round-trip for every new product.

Works well with:

- Seasonal badges (`SALE`, `NEW`, `限时`)
- Localized price and currency
- The same crop for list, detail, and ads
